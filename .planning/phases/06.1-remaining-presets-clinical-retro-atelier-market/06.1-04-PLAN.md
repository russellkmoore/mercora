<objective>
Close the phase on evidence. Four presets exist and validate; this plan proves they look right, that
the three light ones survive the acid tests the direction doc named for them, and that the admin
Appearance page offers all seven.

Purpose: THEME-05's second half. Plans 06.1-01 through 06.1-03 produced correct data and correct
wiring; correctness of colour values says nothing about whether a near-white surface still shows its
hover states, whether a linen page still separates its cards, or whether a bigger radius reached the
inputs it was chosen for. That only comes from looking, deliberately, and writing down what was seen.

Output: four canonical capture grids, a findings table carrying every judgement including the ones
that changed nothing, the answered UI-consideration rows, a per-preset backlog record, and the
phase-close roll-up with THEME-05 marked complete.
