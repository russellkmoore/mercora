<objective>
Fix the backwards gift-card capability gate and stand up the one visibility predicate every
later plan imports.

Today `resolveCommerceCapabilities` wraps the gift-card capability in an acquisition gate, so
turning selling off also stops a shopper redeeming a card they already paid for. Honor is what
governs redemption (D-03). This plan makes tender follow honor, keeps the sell-without-honor
startup throw exactly as it is (D-02, GCF-04), and rewrites the test that currently pins the
backwards behaviour into a four-state matrix.

It also creates `lib/gift-cards/visibility.ts` — the shared predicate that plans 13-02, 13-03,
13-04 and 13-08 all call, so the "is this gift card publicly visible" rule is written once
(D-14).

Purpose: prove the flag semantics end to end on one thin slice before any surface work starts.
Output: a corrected capability resolver, a four-state matrix test, a predicate module, its unit test.
</objective>
