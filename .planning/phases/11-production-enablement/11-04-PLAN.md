<assumption_delta_decision>
The phase's primary noun changed during discussion from "a key" to **a versioned key ring**, and
there are two instances of it: the gift-card code HMAC ring and the delivery ring.

**Decision: no-change.** Both rings already share one shape — a JSON object keyed by canonical
positive integers, a separate current-version pointer, and a bounded-ring parser — implemented
twice in `lib/gift-cards/config.ts` and frozen by the tests extended in 11-01. This phase
provisions two instances of that shape. It does not generalise the abstraction, does not merge the
parsers, and does not touch `lib/gift-cards/`.

The invariant that keeps the claim honest lives in 11-01 Task 2 and 11-02 Task 2: both parsers
accept the exact placeholder shapes `.env.example` documents and reject a key one byte under the
floor. If a future phase does unify the two rings, those invariants are what must keep passing.
</assumption_delta_decision>
