<objective>
Lay the phase's telemetry foundation and prove it end to end on the tax-fallback path: register
every taxonomy entry the phase needs in both parity files, emit `checkout.tax_fallback` at the exact
line where checkout degrades to the flat rate, and make the two allocation functions importable so
plan `02-04` can table-test them.

Purpose: `lib/services/checkout-pricing.ts:712` silently swallows a Stripe Tax failure and prices
the order at the configured flat rate. An operator has no way to know it happened. OBS-01 makes that
degradation visible without leaking a single identifier.

Output: three new registered events, two new enum values, one new enum field, one `recordTelemetry`
call site, two new exports, and unit tests that fail if the event stops firing, starts firing on the
success path, or ever carries an identifier.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
