<objective>
Make checkout ask for what the cart actually needs. An all-digital cart collects billing details and pays; it never sees a shipping address step or a shipping method step. A cart with anything physical in it behaves exactly as it does today.

Purpose: this is the observation Russell made on 2026-09-08 — a cart holding only a gift card still walked him through shipping options. It is also the last unshipped half of SHOP-05: the confirmation screen needs to show what was bought after the cart has been cleared.

Output: one new pure module holding the three facts that define the digital path; a rewired step machine in CheckoutClient that reads them; a cart snapshot handed to the confirmation modal; two new test files; and the phase gate.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
The orchestrator's assumption-delta detector fired on this phase's scope, on the signals "carts that also hold physical items", "name, message, delivery date optional", and "choose Send to myself".

**The noun that is now primary:** the cart's **fulfilment mix** — whether every line is digital or at least one line is physical. Before this phase there was no such noun; checkout assumed shipping unconditionally and every step list was the shipping-first one.

**Decision: promote.** The checkout step list is derived from the cart's fulfilment mix by a generalised function, `isDigitalOnlyCart`, exported from `lib/checkout/digital-only.ts`. The physical flow is one branch of that function's result, not a default with a digital exception welded onto its side. Concretely, that means:

- The three facts that define a digital checkout — the predicate, the shipping method id, and the step labels — live in one module, not scattered as inline literals across the step machine.
- The physical branch is the else of a single decision evaluated once per render, and stays byte-identical.
- Nothing keys off a product id, a URL segment, or a hardcoded "is this the gift card" check.

**Cart line identity: no-change.** `lib/gift-cards/line-identity.ts` already generalises line identity over the customization, and `tests/unit/lib/stores/cart-store-lines.test.ts` already proves merge-and-separate. Nothing there needs promoting; nothing there is touched.

**Invariant test (adopted, Task 1):** for every cart composition built from gift-card lines and physical lines, `isDigitalOnlyCart` over the cart items agrees with the negation of `hasPhysicalCheckoutLines` over the matching order lines. This encodes the assumption that the two independent signals — a customization on the client line and a digital fulfilment type on the server line — describe the same fulfilment mix, and it will fail loudly on the day a non-gift-card digital product ships.
</assumption_delta_decision>
